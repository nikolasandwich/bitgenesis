# V3 study001: exploratory lineage removal and lagged deposit replay

Preregistered before executing seeds88000..88004. This is a bounded mechanism
assay, not a graduation of the failed viability pilots or a cooperation test.
Use random founders with V3 defaults except16x16 sites,32 founder attempts,
feeding_limit16 and renewal_per_thousand60. Recycling stays1, initial A12/B0,
energy640, birth threshold1280, mutation100. This energetic setting is chosen
for exploratory interaction opportunity, not because it passed pilot viability.

For each of five sources, record and independently audit a300-step prefix.
Among lineages with living members, choose the founder lineage with greatest
cumulative retained B release during the prefix; tie-break by smallest founder
ID. Require at least two living founder lineages and positive target release.
Otherwise report unavailable, with no replacement source or forced target.
Target choice uses only the prefix. It does not require proven cross-feeding.

Continue each available origin for500 steps in three branches: intact, target
lineage removed, and target removed plus lagged B deposit replay. Removal at the
boundary after300 exports all energy of living target descendants, frees their
sites and leaves legacy resource deposits. The intact branch fixes donor B
release schedule: retained releases at tick t are proposed at the same site
before t+1. Reference final-tick releases are outside the horizon and omitted.
Retain proposed, accepted and capacity-rejected imports. Do not adapt schedules
to treatment outcomes. Record common state and reference-schedule hashes.

Primary descriptive contrasts are other-lineage terminal population and new
births after the prefix: removed minus intact, and replayed minus removed.
Report all per-source contrasts, zero outcomes, unavailable sources and energy
exports/imports/rejections. No significance threshold or positive-result claim.
Removing target individuals changes competition, occupancy and shuffled actor
order; B replay is not an exact restoration of a live partner. Positive recovery
would not alone prove evolved cooperation, reciprocal benefit or stable niches.

Gate: complete five-source coverage, exact configuration and target selection,
prefix independent audit, common-origin equality, reference schedule binding,
independent boundary reconstruction and same-engine branch replay. The latter
does not independently establish all intervened world dynamics; publish that
limitation. Initialization/mutation RNG are not independently audited in prefixes.

Use clean launch/protocol hashes. No more than15 branches,500 steps each, with
128000 worst-case actors per branch and76800 per prefix. Regenerating each
available origin costs another300 steps; branch replay checks cost another1500
steps per available source. Record this verification overhead separately from
logical experimental trajectories. Check8GiB between sources; one bounded source
may overshoot. Stop incomplete on error/limit and preserve records. No replacement,
changed horizons, or additional parameter grid in this study.
