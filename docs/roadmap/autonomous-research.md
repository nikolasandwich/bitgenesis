# Autonomous research mandate

Updated 2026-09-14 by explicit user instruction. This supersedes the previous
V0-only implementation scope. It authorizes implementation, experiments,
analysis and improvements across V1, V2, V3, V4 and later open-ended-evolution
research. It does not assert that any later stage already works or has graduated.

## Working objective

Explore **Simple Rules + Energy + Information + Time -> ?**, designing the world
more than the life. Make routine research and implementation decisions
autonomously, record their rationale and limits, and continue until the user
explicitly stops or pauses the work. A progress question is not a stop request.
When the user begins acceptance review, summarize and await their direction.

## Immediate sequence

1. Independently verify the completed campaign-024 grid and publish its complete
   results, including null or negative findings. Preserve the V0 engine.
2. Finalize the V1 engineering contract and implement the minimal inherited
   sensor-to-action controller in a separate stage package. Preserve the V0 CLI.
3. Verify accounting, inheritance, intervention semantics and deterministic
   replay. Run a bounded viability pilot before freezing formal evaluation.
4. Predeclare independent training and held-out evaluation, ancestor/randomized
   controls, sensory ablations, sampling, budgets and interpretation criteria.
5. Analyze results and choose a bounded improvement or the next stage's minimal
   research prototype. Do not default to another V0 campaign.

## Stage progression

- V1: inherited controllers; distinguish sensory response, reproductive value,
  and evolutionary improvement relative to actual ancestors.
- V2: genome -> development -> organism; compare with direct encoding under
  matched budgets, retaining failed development and its costs.
- V3: ecological interactions; account for resources, test mechanisms with
  perturbations, distinguish imposed niches from evolved differentiation.
- V4: local interacting units; relax explicit organism assumptions one at a
  time and independently measure boundaries, persistence and reproduction.
- Later: bounded tests of continuing innovation, with predeclared measures,
  controls and increasing horizons; never certify unlimited evolution.

The existing roadmap's graduation criteria remain the evidence standards.
An exploratory next-stage prototype may proceed while earlier scientific
questions remain open, with its status and unmet criteria explicit. A null
result is retained; neither endless repetition seeking a positive result nor
adding complexity alone constitutes progress.

## Reproducibility and operating limits

Keep original stage semantics, experiment definitions, seeds, versions and
reproduction routes. Separate pilots from confirmatory evaluations. Record
failures and all preregistered cases. Bound compute, storage and recorded detail
before each campaign. Reuse running jobs rather than launching duplicates.
Prioritize substantive research increments over packaging-only cycles. Verify,
commit and push completed increments; provide milestone updates rather than
unchanged-status notifications. No new paid services are implied by this scope.
