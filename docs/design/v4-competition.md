# Two-founder program competition

`v4-competition-1` starts two explicit immutable programs, with equal initial
material and energy, at two distinct specified sites. Both share the existing
hereditary world, raw resource stocks, input and interaction rules. Mutation is
fixed to0. `competition.run` records ordinary physical/hereditary transitions;
no role or founder identity is read by the dynamics. Program/site tuple order
defines observer roles only. There is no special combat or imposed winner rule.

`competition_audit.audit` independently reconstructs both declared founders and
all subsequent events. `competition_lineage.trace` applies the passive birth
observer and counts living descendants by the actual initial founder mapped to
each role, including the founder itself while alive. Same-program unrelated
founders remain distinct. Starting sites need not be sorted; role order is
preserved separately from ascending-site birth identity assignment.

Engineering seeds99000/99001 cover independently audited dynamics, zero horizon,
invalid coincident sites and program inheritance. For identical programs, swapping
role sites preserves all physical trajectory bytes while exchanging every per-role
count. A single stochastic neutral run need not split population equally. Its
role-swapped pair cancels spatial/random asymmetry without imposing equality on
the engine. Different-program swaps are controlled comparisons, not an assertion
that their physical states remain identical.

This measures competition for occupied sites and shared local resources under the
specified rules. The two-founder state differs from the mixed training ecology.
Even a consistent advantage would not establish group reproduction or general
fitness. All fixed sampled pairs, including neutral and unfavorable ones, belong
in the next registered experiment.
